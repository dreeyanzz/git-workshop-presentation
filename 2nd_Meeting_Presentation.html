Member Engagement

Meeting Attendance

Core Standard

Punctuality and active presence during coordination meetings ensure committee decisions are finalized rapidly without delays.

Proactive Updates

Communication

Share continuous progress updates freely in committee group chats. Don't wait for formal meetings to highlight blockers or ask for help.

Decision Protocol

01

Single Proposal

Auto-Approved

If a lead presents a single well-structured recommendation with no objections, that proposal is automatically approved as the official decision.

02

Counter-Proposals

Justification Needed

Anyone proposing an alternative path must present a logically sound justification covering feasibility, cost, and team impact.

03

Multiple Options

Immediate Vote

When multiple valid options exist, the floor proceeds directly to a vote. Majority decision is final and all members commit to it.
